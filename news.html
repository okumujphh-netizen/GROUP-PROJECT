Featured Story

Victory FC Go Top After Impressive Away Victory

23 August 2026

Victory FC continued their perfect start to the 2026/27 National League season with a convincing 3-0 victory over Coastal Warriors. Goals from Daniel Mwangi, Brian Kamau and Kevin Otieno secured all three points and moved Victory FC to the top of the league table after Matchday 2. The victory follows an opening-day 2-1 win over Nairobi United, giving the club six points from its opening two fixtures.

Victory FC Ready for Highland Stars Challenge

26 August 2026

Victory FC return to Victory Stadium this weekend as they welcome Highland Stars in Matchday 3 of the National League.
